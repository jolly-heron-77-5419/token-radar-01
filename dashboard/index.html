How the implied value is worked out

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live protocols in the same category, read off this snapshot. A DEX dollar and a lending dollar do not price the same, and the snapshot's own live protocols say by how much. Categories with fewer than three live samples fall back to the snapshot-wide median.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −60%, +120%) × 0.6. Momentum counts, but a single week cannot triple a value.
  • Maturity — 0.7 + min(age, 3 years) / 3 years × 0.5. A protocol tracked for years with no token is further along the road to one.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.02 per chain, up to 10 chains. A protocol live on many chains has more surface to launch from.

The implied value is this entry's own claim, never make.fun's: it says what a token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. A protocol is shown as recording no token, never as having none: its DefiLlama record may simply not carry one.
